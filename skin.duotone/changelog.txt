v0.9.0 (2026-10-18)
- First build of Duotone, forked from the Majan 1.0.2 engine (OSD auto-hide, play curtain, track pickers,
  OSD settings tabs, context-menu centring, empty-folder focus) and redesigned throughout.
- One section system ($VAR[SectionColor] + text / tint variables): Films vermilion, Series cobalt,
  Music acid, Search / Settings paper. Every field, print, rule and label recolours together.
- Monotone prints (colordiffuse + halftone), the focused item in full colour with a 6% zoom and an 8px
  ink frame; TMDb Helper black-and-white prints for the Home spotlight and the info fanart.
- Home: Anton section stack with a 200 ms field wipe, spotlight + rows + vertical All tile per section;
  the Search section is the system hub.
- Views: 7-column poster wall with Anton masthead and numerals, episodes with giant numerals, list with
  print panel, albums wall with the focused album's live tracklist.
- Poster-style info page, music info, now playing, OSD / seek bar / fullscreen info on section slabs,
  dialogs, keyboard, settings, skin settings (9 settings + reset), search with per-section result rows.

Known gaps
- Spotlight black-and-white print only for the Films spotlight (TMDb Helper follows one widget container).
- Up from deep in the wall walks row by row to the header (Kodi container behaviour, as in Majan).
- PVR windows are minimal (no PVR on Omar's box); Timers.xml not provided.
